History of Bekasi City

Bekasi, which was originally part of the Tarumanegara Kingdom in the 5th century, is one of the fastest growing cities in Indonesia. Its name comes from the Sanskrit word “bhagasasi”, meaning “sun”, symbolizing the vibrancy and vitality of the city. The city has played a strategic role in Indonesian history, especially as a key location in the struggle for independence against Dutch colonialism in the 20th century. Where is Bekasi City Located

Bekasi is a city in the province of West Java, Indonesia, and is located east of Jakarta. The city is in the lowlands, directly bordering DKI Jakarta to the west, and is one of the largest satellite cities in the Jabodetabek area. Bekasi is known as an industrial and residential city, and has many residential areas and modern shopping centers. Its proximity to Jakarta makes it a preferred residence for many workers who work in the capital city. The development of transportation infrastructure such as toll roads and commuter trains has further improved Bekasi's connectivity with surrounding cities.
